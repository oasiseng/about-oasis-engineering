# Oasis Engineering services

Each lane below has one best starting link. Published prices and turnarounds live in [llms.txt](https://oasisengineering.com/llms.txt) and [/api/catalog](https://oasisengineering.com/api/catalog); the intake shows the typical range before anyone submits. Prices quoted here were current when this file was last updated and the site wins if they differ.

## Engineer letters

The broadest lane. One intake handles permit and inspector requests, after-the-fact and unpermitted work, covered work, missed inspections, structural changes and component letters. A few taps route each case to the right path, and photos and documents are uploaded right there.

- Typically $295–$1,500, scoped per case. Typically 1–2 business days once we have what we need.
- Start: [/contact/?service=letter.general](https://oasisengineering.com/contact/?service=letter.general)
- Overview: [oasisengineering.com/engineer-letters/](https://oasisengineering.com/engineer-letters/)
- Engineer-letter cases are handled under **EngineerLetters.co**, powered by Oasis Engineering.

More detail: [engineerletters.md](engineerletters.md).

## Wind calculations and wind engineering

Its own lane, even when someone calls it a letter. It covers ASCE 7 wind loads and design pressures, product approval gaps and missing NOAs, and signed and sealed drawings for openings, cladding and roof-mounted solar.

- Typically $1,000–$2,000. 2–3 business days.
- Start: [windcalculations.com/contact](https://windcalculations.com/contact), or a packaged product from [wind-packages.md](wind-packages.md).

## Energy calculations

Manual J & S load calcs and Florida energy forms (R402/R405, EPL card), with Manual D duct design when there are ducts.

- From $100. 1–3 business days. At the set price the intake goes straight to checkout.
- Start: [/contact/?service=calc.energy](https://oasisengineering.com/contact/?service=calc.energy)
